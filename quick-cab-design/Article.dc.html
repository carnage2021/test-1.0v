Statue of Unity day trip from Vadodara: timings, route and tips

By The Quick Cab team12 Sep 20266 min read

The Statue of Unity at Kevadia is about 90 km from Vadodara, a two-hour drive through Dabhoi. You can see the statue, the viewing gallery and one or two nearby attractions in a single day if you start early.

When to leave Vadodara

Leave by 7 am to reach as the ticket counters open and before tour buses arrive. The statue is closed on Mondays, so plan for another day of the week.

What to see in one day

Viewing gallery

Book the viewing gallery slot online. Morning slots have shorter queues and clearer views of the Sardar Sarovar dam.

Valley of Flowers and Jungle Safari

Both are a short drive from the statue. Your driver waits at each stop, so you do not need to arrange local transport.

One way or round trip?

If you are coming back the same evening, a round trip keeps the car and driver with you all day. If you are staying overnight at Kevadia, book one way and pay for the drive there only.

Quick checklist

  • Tickets booked online, saved on your phone
  • Water, a cap and comfortable shoes
  • ID for every adult in the group
